# Installing Itheros

Itheros is a fantasy RPG for Android powered by your real steps. It isn't in the Play Store, so you install it
from here. It takes about five minutes.

## What you need
- An Android phone with **Android 9 or newer** (Settings → About phone → Android version).
- **Health Connect**, the place where Android keeps your step count:
  - Android 14 and newer: it's built in.
  - Android 9 to 13: install [Health Connect](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.healthdata)
    from the Play Store.
- Something that counts your steps into Health Connect. Many phones do it by themselves; otherwise a step app
  (Google Fit, Samsung Health, Fitbit, or your watch's app) with "share with Health Connect" switched on.

## Way 1: with Obtainium (you're told about updates)
Obtainium is a free app that installs apps straight from where they're published and tells you when there's a
new version.

1. On your phone, open [Obtainium's download page](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium/releases/latest) and
   download the file that ends in `arm64-v8a-release.apk` (it fits almost every phone). Open it.
2. Your phone says it isn't allowed to install apps from this source. Tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this
   source**, go back, and tap **Install**.
3. Open Obtainium, tap **Add app**, paste `https://github.com/Zkalf/Itheros` and tap **Add**.
4. Tap **Install**. Allow Obtainium to install apps the same way as in step 2.

Updates: Obtainium shows a notification when a new version is out. Tap **Update**. Your game is kept.

## Way 2: the plain link (no extra app)
1. On your phone, open
   [github.com/Zkalf/Itheros/releases/latest/download/itheros.apk](https://github.com/Zkalf/Itheros/releases/latest/download/itheros.apk).
2. Open the downloaded file. Your phone says it isn't allowed to install apps from this source: tap **Settings**,
   switch on **Allow from this source**, go back, and tap **Install**.

Updates: when there's a new version, open the same link again and install over the top. Your game is kept.
Don't uninstall first: that deletes your game.

## "Unknown app" warnings
Google Play Protect may say the app is from an unknown developer or wants to scan it. That's because it isn't in
the Play Store. Tap **More details** → **Install anyway** (or **Scan app**, then install).

## First start
Tap **Allow step access** and allow **Steps** in Health Connect. Steps you walked before installing don't count;
from now on, every step does.

## Good to know
- Your step data stays on your phone. The game has no internet access and sends nothing anywhere.
- The game isn't copied to your Google backup, so a new phone means a fresh start.
- Something wrong? Send me the version number (Character → Settings, at the bottom) and a screenshot.
