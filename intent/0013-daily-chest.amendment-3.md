# Intent 0013 amendment 3: rarer chest gear, Crafting materials

Status: accepted
Amends: [intent 0013](0013-daily-chest.md) outcome 2 (gear in about 1 chest in 5);
[amendment 1](0013-daily-chest.amendment-1.md) point 8 (gear in about 1 chest in 5, a legendary in about 1 chest in
500)
Issue: #57
Product owner: Zkalf (chose on 2026-11-01, after the sim of plan 0037 M1: gear in 1 chest in 10, and Crafting
materials as a choice of the common roll)
Date: 2026-11-01

## Why
- The sim of plan 0037 showed that a pure fighter gets almost all its gear from chests.
- With gear in 1 chest in 5, a fighter that never gathers or crafts reached level 30 on day 86. The walker with
  professions reached it on day 85.
- Spec 0010 wants professions to matter: the fighter at least 15% later.
- The owner wants Crafting to get more out of the chest.

## Change
1. **Gear in about 1 chest in 10**, not 1 in 5.
2. With the start quality chances of amendment 1, about 1 chest in 1,000 gives a legendary piece.
3. **Crafting materials:** a player who has learned Crafting gets one more choice in the common roll.
4. The Crafting choice gives the inputs of one craft of a gear recipe at the player's Crafting level.
5. The chase items of amendment 1 stay out: no inputs for their recipes.
6. Everything else in intent 0013 and amendments 1 and 2 stays as it is.

## Balance cost
- Chest gear is half as common for everyone.
- A crafter gets materials for one more craft in some chests. The fighter at level 30 is about 19% later than the
  walker in the sim.

## What it means for the specs
- Spec 0013 amendment 1 sets the gear chance, the Crafting choice, and the pacing targets that the chest moves.
