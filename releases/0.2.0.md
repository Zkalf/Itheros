The first version for friends. Welcome to Itheros!

- Every step you walk powers your hero: fight goblins, skeletons and zombies, mine ore and craft gear.
- Climb levels, find gear in five qualities from Common to Legendary, and open the map beyond the river at level 10.
- The journal tells you what happened while you were away; trophies mark your milestones.
- Your step data stays on your phone. The game has no internet access at all.
