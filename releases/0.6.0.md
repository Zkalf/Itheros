See what changed after each update, change when an action stops, and smoother screens.

- **New in Itheros:** after an update, the game shows you what changed, like these lines. Skipped a version? You
  see its notes too. Read them again any time in Settings.
- **Change when an action stops:** tap the current action, or one that waits in the queue, to change its stop
  condition. It goes on where it was, with nothing lost.
- **Type the number:** tap the number of "Until I have N" or "Until N crafted" to type it, instead of tapping + many
  times.
- **Smoother screens:** a new screen now slides in from the side, without the small stutter of before. On the World
  map, the list of a place and the Start sheet now open and close smoothly, without a lag.
- **Hunting and fishing:** "Until I have N" now lists only what you have already caught at that spot.
- **App icon:** a thinner dark edge around the picture on your home screen.
- **Fixed:** in "Until I have N", the suggested number now follows the item you pick: what you have of it, plus 5.
- Your step data still stays on your phone. The game has no internet access at all.
