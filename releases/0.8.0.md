A daily supply chest, a bank at Camp, a bag limit and a new fight log.

- **Supply chest:** each day you spend 5,000 steps on actions (fights, gathering, crafting, walks), you earn a supply
  chest. Steps that go into the step bank don't count, but the step bank's boost does. Open it from the new chest
  button left of Achievements: it always holds materials or meals, sometimes a piece of gear near your level that
  rolls better quality than a monster drop, and rarely a boss shard. Chests wait for you, so days away still count,
  and "Open all" opens them together. What's inside is set the moment you earn it.
- **Item bank:** Camp has a bank for items. Store items there and take them back from the Bank card on Now, or look
  inside from the Bank button under your bag anywhere. Crafting and cooking at Camp use what's in the bank too.
- **Bag limit:** your bag holds 40 kinds of item. Each kind over 40 makes fights, gathering and walks cost 5% more
  steps, up to 50%. Crafting, cooking and rerolls at Camp cost no more. Store items in the bank at Camp to make room.
  The bag shows its spaces, and Now tells you when you're over. The new Utility talent Bigger bag gives 2 more spaces
  per rank.
- **New fight log:** the Now screen shows less during a fight: your health, the monster's health, how the last fight
  ended, and a Fight details button. Fight details lists your last 10 fights (rounds, damage taken, crits) and the
  latest fight round by round, from the top down. Crits, blocks, dodges and misses each have their own colour.
- **Fixed:** you can now plan a walk back to the place you stand at. Queue goblins and mining from Camp, then open
  Camp on the World map: "Walk here" adds the walk home to the end of the queue. Its steps count from where the queue
  ends.
- **Fixed:** queued rerolls no longer get stuck when your dust runs out (or sits in the bank). The rerolls without
  dust are skipped, and the report says why.
