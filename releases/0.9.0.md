Pictures for many things in your bag, Salvage all, and rerolls at Camp with the dust in the bank.

- **New pictures:** many things now have their own pictures in your bag:
  - every pickaxe, hatchet, hammer, rod, bow and pot, from the old tools to mithril,
  - oak, ash and pine logs, and the rare heartwoods,
  - all 16 fish, from perch to the legends of the deep,
  - venison, boar, elk and ibex meat, grouse, the three hides and the three fish scales,
  - the first meals (grilled perch, pike steak, venison skewer, boar stew, golden carp curry, the Mossback feast),
  - all five dusts, the three gems and the three boss shards,
  - early gear: the copper and bone spears, mace and club, the garnet ring, the oak charm, the deer-hide gloves,
    bracers and belt, and the lake-scale greaves.
- **Professions show their tools:** each profession now shows a picture of its tool on the Professions tab, on its own
  page and next to its teacher at Camp.
- **Salvage all:** at a forge, the Bag has a button for each quality, such as "Salvage all Common (14)". The item card
  has "Salvage all" for a stack of the same item. Each asks once, then salvages them all.
- **Rerolls at Camp:** a reroll at Camp now uses the dust in the bank too, as crafting does. It takes the dust in your
  bag first. Away from Camp, a reroll still needs the dust in your bag.
- **Queued rerolls say so:** a reroll you add from an item card while something runs now shows "Added to the queue",
  as other actions do.
- **Character opens on Bag:** the Character tab now opens on your bag. Gear is one tap away.
- **Square level badges:** level badges are squares now, the skull too.
- **Fixed:** the item card no longer lets you queue more rerolls than your dust can pay. When the rerolls already in
  your queue take the dust, Reroll is greyed out and says so.
- **Fixed:** salvage messages no longer pile up. After quick salvages, the message on screen is always the last one.
