Quests and the people of the south: they talk, they have work for you, and the lore of each place waits to be found.

- **Quests:** Odda, the teachers, Pell, Aldous and Jory now ask for your help. Take a quest on their sheet, do the
  task, and hand it in for XP and a supply chest.
  - A gold "!" marks someone with work for you, and a gold "?" a quest ready to hand in.
  - The quest card on Now shows your quests and their counts, in the place of "Next goal".
  - The Journal has a Quests tab with your open and finished quests.
- **Meet the people of the south:** Odda at Camp, Pell the charcoal burner in Goblin Woods, Aldous the sexton at the
  Old Graveyard, and someone shady at a hidden cove. What they say changes as you go. Beat the Graveyard King, and
  the teachers and Ysolde at Camp have something new to say about it.
- **Portraits:** the people, the teachers at Camp and Ysolde have portraits, in their rows and at the top of their
  sheets.
- **Lore to find:** lore pages turn up on your first visit to a place, from what you do, from the people, and now and
  then while you fight or gather. The Lore tab in the Journal groups the lore by place, and six new trophies wait
  for those who find all the lore of a place south of the river.
- **Select several items:** hold an item in your bag or in the bank to select it, then tap more items. Store, take or
  salvage them all in one go.
- **A simpler Gear page:** no figure, and your 16 gear slots stand in a grid of four columns, all on one screen. An
  empty slot shows a faint outline of what goes there (a helm, a boot, a ring), so you can tell the slots apart at a
  glance.
- **Drawn icons** for the talents, the Bank, the Forge, the Camp fire, the trainer on Now, and the news on a level-up.
- **Fixed:** back from the Forge or the Camp fire's screen, opened from Now, takes you back to Now.
