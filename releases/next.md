A big update: your own character, six professions, a lake to fish and a whole new look.

- **Who are you?** Name your character, pick male, female or neither, and choose one of six origins. Your origin's profession starts at level 5, and its teacher greets you as kin. Your progress stays: you see this screen once.
- **Six professions:** Mining, Woodcutting, Crafting, Fishing, Hunting and Cooking, each with its own level and tool. Learn them from the teachers in camp.
- **Mirror Lake and the hunt:** fish for record catches, track deer in the woods, and cook meals for a short boost. Somewhere out there, a message in a bottle waits…
- **Fights with depth:** swords, spears and maces, misses, crits, dodges and blocks. Monsters wear armour that some weapons beat. A bestiary and lore notes tell you which.
- **A new look:** four screens (Now, World, Professions, Character), a drawn map and a paper doll with 16 gear slots. Everywhere is open from day one, so explore at your own risk.
- Better gear gets bonus stats. Break down spare gear for dust and reroll its quality.
- Your step data still stays on your phone. The game has no internet access at all.
