Talents, and new tips from the hunter and the fisherman.

- **Talents:** from level 10, each level gives a talent point. Spend them on Character → Talents, in two trees:
  Combat (faster fights and better loot) and Utility (a bigger bank, cheaper gathering, crafting and travel, an
  extra queue slot). Every 5 points in a tree open its next row. Ysolde, the Guild trainer in camp, lets you reset
  your talents and spend the points again: free the first time.
- **Hunting and fishing tips:** Sigrun and Wen in camp now tell you what lives at each hunting spot and each water,
  and whisper about the rare ones. Wen has a story about one water you have to find first.
- **Low XP:** a monster far below your level (grey) now says "Low XP" on its row. It still gives 5% of its XP, and
  at least 1.
- **Swipe back:** on a screen that slides in, such as a profession, the Journal or Settings, swipe to the right
  anywhere to go back. Taps and scrolling work as before.
- **Less text:** the step bank explains itself when you tap it, and the hints under it, at the bottom of Now and
  under the World map are gone.
- **Fixed:** in the Journal, the tab you swipe to now lights up while you swipe, not only after.
