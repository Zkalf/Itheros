Tougher fights, the Graveyard King at the river, the bank one tap away, a stack limit in the bag, and a clearer Character screen.

- **Tougher fights:** monsters hit harder and last longer, and each kill gives more XP and loot. Hits miss more
  often, so luck matters again, even with the best gear. The first monsters stay gentle.
- **The Graveyard King guards the river:** beat him once to reach Grey Moor and the lands beyond. If you have already
  been to Grey Moor, the road stays open for you.
- **A stack limit in the bag:** one bag space holds up to 100 of a material or 20 of a meal. A bigger pile takes more
  spaces, and the bag shows each stack as its own square. If your bag holds big piles, it may be overloaded after the
  update: store them in the bank at Camp, and nothing is lost.
- **The bank one tap away:**
  - a Bank button stands in the top bar, on every tab;
  - at Camp, "Store all materials" moves every material from your bag to the bank in one tap;
  - each gear or tool row in the bank shows its stats and bonus stats under its name, so you can tell two alike pieces
    apart.
- **A clearer Character screen:**
  - your level stands beside your name, and your seven stats stand in one row, so Gear, Bag and Talents start higher;
  - Hit now shows your chance to hit a monster of your level, for example 77%;
  - tap a stat (HP, Attack, Defense, Crit, Hit, Dodge or Block) to see what it does and where its number comes from.
- **At Camp, the services first:** on the Now screen the Bank, the Forge and the Camp fire stand before the teachers.
- **Recipes you can make come first:** the recipe lists of Crafting and Cooking start with the highest level you can
  make. The recipes you cannot make yet come last, the next one to open first.
- **Smaller changes:**
  - XP numbers count inside the level, like their bars: a new level starts at "0 / 48 XP";
  - a walk that both used and saved step bank steps reports one line, for example "Step bank: +2,630 steps.";
  - a locked recipe or spot no longer repeats its name: "Needs Crafting 16 (you have 11)";
  - on a first visit, the story of a place stands under your current action, so you see your progress first;
  - the Camp tour's last card names the Bank button.
- **A smaller download:** about 27 MB, less than half of before. A phone with only a 32-bit processor can no longer
  install the game.
