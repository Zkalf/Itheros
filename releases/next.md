A big update: a new look with pictures, walks in your queue, and 30 achievements that give real rewards.

- **A new look:** a dark teal theme with pictures for monsters, gear, places and gathering spots, a new app icon and a
  welcome screen. Things without a picture yet show a placeholder marked "PH". More pictures will come.
- **Each profession has its own screen.** Open Professions and tap one.
- **Walks in your queue:** a walk to another place now takes a slot in your queue, just like a fight or a craft. Tap a
  place on the map and choose "Walk here". The walk bar shows how many steps are left. "Add to queue" tells you what
  it added.
- **30 achievements:** Early, Mid and Late. Each one shows its reward.
  - **Milestones** come on the way and give a bigger step bank, or a piece of gear.
  - **Feats** take a long grind and give a lasting stat bonus. Chieftain's Bane gives you a 6th queue slot.
- **Old achievements are checked again.** When you open the game, it checks each achievement by its new goal. If you
  no longer meet one, you lose it and its reward, and the report tells you what you lost. You can earn it again.
- **The away report** draws your route on a small map.
- Your step data still stays on your phone. The game has no internet access at all.
