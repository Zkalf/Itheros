A camp tour for new players, meals worth cooking again, search and sort in the bag and the bank, and a picture for every item.

- **Meals are worth cooking again:** every meal now lasts 6,000 steps (a perfect dish 12,000), and Attack and HP
  meals also make each fight cost fewer steps: 12%, 20% or 28% by tier.
- **A tour of the camp:** after you make your character, Odda the quartermaster shows you around the game in six
  short cards: Now, the World map, the professions, your gear and talents, the bag and the bank, and the buttons at
  the top. Skip it at any card, or play it again from Settings.
- **Find things in the bag and the bank:**
  - buttons above the bag and both bank tabs show All, Gear, Tools, Meals or Materials;
  - below them, type part of a name (or a quality, like "rare") to find an item;
  - sort the list by bag order, name, count or quality;
  - each list remembers its kind and its sort until you close the game.
- **The bank shows what you store:** each item in the bank has its picture, and a tap opens its card with its stats,
  so you can tell two alike items apart before you store or take them. Bank and Bag are buttons, and the screen opens
  on Bank.
- **Until the bag is full:** fights and gathering have a new stop choice. The action stops when your bag has no free
  space left, before it gets heavy.
- **Destroy items:** a full bag? Open an item's card in the bag or the bank and tap Destroy to get rid of it for good,
  anywhere. The game asks first, and for a stack it asks how many.
- **A picture for everything you carry:**
  - all gear, from the iron, mithril and drake-fang weapons to the rings, the totem, the idol, the hide sets, the
    greaves and the mantles, the bone greataxe and the golden-antler mantle,
  - every meal, from smoked trout to the Pale One feast,
  - the rare hunting finds: the golden antler, Old Tusk's tusk and the snow lynx pelt,
  - each vein, tree, water and trail, on Now and on the World map,
  - the 19 achievements that showed a stand-in now have their own sewn patch,
  - Mirror Lake and Smugglers' Cove have a map marker: a fish over the waves.
- **Pictures where you plan:**
  - each action on the Now screen's queue shows a picture of its monster, spot, item or place, with its number on the
    corner; tapping a spot or a recipe also shows its picture at the top,
  - each recipe shows a picture of what it makes, and in a recipe's sheet each material has its picture; a tap on it
    tells you where to find it,
  - each monster in the Journal's Bestiary has its picture, with its level next to its name.
- **Clearer locked spots:** a spot or recipe you can't do yet says your level too, for example "needs Woodcutting 8
  (you have 5)".
- **App icon:** the emblem now fills the round icon, with no black border.
- **Fixed:** a new game now gets the steps you walked earlier that day.
- **Fixed:** a reroll on the queue no longer shows "Until 1 crafted".
