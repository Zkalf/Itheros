Clearer buttons, a quicker look at your gear, and crafting that tells you what you have.

- **Clearer buttons:** the button you picked now has a teal fill and white text, so you can see your choice at a
  glance: Gear or Bag, the stop choices, the crafting filters, the Journal tabs and the bottom bar. The text of most
  buttons has a thin black outline. The teal main buttons, such as Start, keep their plain dark text.
- **Your gear in one tap:** tap a worn item on your character to see its card. "Change" shows what in your bag fits
  that slot. The list of a slot now also shows the bonus stats of what you wear, such as "+3% Crit".
- **Crafting tells you what you have:** each recipe says how many times you can make it ("Enough for 4"). Before you
  start, a table shows each material: what you have, what the craft uses and what is left, counted after your queue.
- **Clearer words:** tools and meals show what they do to your step cost: "−10% steps" saves steps, "Full step cost"
  saves none. Weapon recipes say their damage type and hands ("Slash · One hand").
- **Profession screens:** the name shows once, at the top. On Crafting and Cooking, the recipe filters show once you
  have learned the profession.
- **World map:** names stay next to their own place and no longer jump when a new place appears.
- **Bigger pictures** on the Achievements screen.
- **Large text:** with a very large text size on your phone, the profession rows now fit on the screen.
- **Fixed:** a meal's card no longer offers "Eat" when you have none of it left.
- Your step data still stays on your phone. The game has no internet access at all.
