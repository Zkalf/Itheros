- **Supply chest:** each day you spend 5,000 steps on actions (fights, gathering, crafting, walks), you earn a supply
  chest. Steps that go into the bank don't count, but the bank's boost does. Open it from the new chest button left
  of Achievements: it always holds materials or meals, sometimes a piece of gear near your level that rolls better
  quality than a monster drop, and rarely a boss shard. Chests wait for you, so days away still count, and "Open all"
  opens them together. What's inside is set the moment you earn it.
- **Fixed:** you can now plan a walk back to the place you stand at. Queue goblins and mining from Camp, then open
  Camp on the World map: "Walk here" adds the walk home to the end of the queue. Its steps count from where the
  queue ends.
- **New fight log:** the Now screen shows less during a fight: your health, the monster's health, how the last fight
  ended, and a Fight details button. Fight details lists your last 10 fights (rounds, damage taken, crits) and the
  latest fight round by round, from the top down. Crits, blocks, dodges and misses each have their own colour.
- **Bank:** Camp has a bank. Store items there and take them back from the Bank card on Now, or look inside from
  the Bank button under your bag anywhere. Crafting and cooking at Camp use what's in the bank too.
