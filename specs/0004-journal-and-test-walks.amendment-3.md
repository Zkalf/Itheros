# Spec 0004 amendment 3: achievements and levels as banners

Status: accepted
Amends: [spec 0004](0004-journal-and-test-walks.md) amendment 2 (the "While you were away" screen) and the journal
Product owner: Zkalf (nearly missed an achievement in the report; chose option b, 2026-10-19)
Date: 2026-10-19

## Change
Achievements and levels reached are no longer plain lines in the report. They show as highlighted banners at the top
of the "While you were away" screen and of each journal entry, one per achievement or level, with an icon:
"Achievement unlocked: Prospector" (trophy), "Reached level 6!" (level icon). The banners use the theme's highlight
colour, not an item quality colour, so orange still means legendary.

## Acceptance criterion
13. A report with an achievement or a level shows each as a banner above the report lines, on the screen and in the
    journal, and not as a plain line.
