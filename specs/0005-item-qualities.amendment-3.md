# Spec 0005 amendment 3: quality colour in reports

Status: accepted
Amends: [spec 0005](0005-item-qualities.md) criterion 9 (as replaced by amendment 2)
Product owner: Zkalf (asked for it after playtesting, 2026-10-19)
Date: 2026-10-19

## Change
In the "While you were away" card and the journal, gear better than common is drawn in its quality colour where a
line names it: "Hide vest (Rare)" in blue. The rest of the line, common gear and materials stay in the normal text
colour.

## Acceptance criterion
12. Report and journal lines draw gear names of uncommon or better in their quality colour, and nothing else.
