# Spec 0006 amendment 1: Craft appears with the first recipe; "Trophies" in the bar

Status: accepted
Amends: [spec 0006](0006-more-screens.md) decision 1, criterion 1
Product owner: Zkalf
Date: 2026-10-19

## Change
1. **Craft is hidden until the first recipe opens** (level 3). Spec 0001 criterion 8 says locked systems are
   hidden, not greyed out; an empty Craft tab at levels 1–2 would be one. Before that the bottom bar has four
   screens: Now · Map · Character · Achievements.
2. **The bottom bar names Achievements "Trophies"**: "Achievements" is too long and wrapped onto two lines on the
   Fairphone. The screen's title stays "Achievements".
3. Wording: the area sheet says "You are here" for the player's own area (§2.2 "here"), and the resting card
   says where to pick actions ("Your steps go to the step bank. Pick something to do on the Map or Craft screen.").

## Acceptance criteria
Criterion 1 becomes: the bottom bar has Now, Map, Craft (from the first recipe), Character and Achievements
(labelled "Trophies"); the app opens on Now; the journal opens from each.
