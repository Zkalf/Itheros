# Spec 0006 amendment 2: Achievements move to a trophy button at the top

Status: accepted
Amends: [spec 0006](0006-more-screens.md) decision 1, criterion 1 (as changed by amendment 1)
Product owner: Zkalf
Date: 2026-10-20

## Change
1. **Achievements leave the bottom bar.** A trophy button sits in the top bar next to the Journal button, on every
   screen, and opens the Achievements screen (unchanged) as its own page with a back arrow, like the Journal.
2. The bottom bar keeps Now · Map · Craft (from the first recipe) · Character, so each tab gets more room.
3. When an achievement is earned that hasn't been looked at yet, the trophy button shows a small dot until the
   Achievements screen is opened.

## Acceptance criteria
Criterion 1 becomes: the bottom bar has Now, Map, Craft (from the first recipe) and Character; the app opens on Now;
the journal and the achievements open from top-bar buttons on each screen; a dot on the trophy button marks an
achievement earned since the screen was last opened.
