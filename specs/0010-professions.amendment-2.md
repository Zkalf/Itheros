# Spec 0010 amendment 2: when the walker crafts tools and rerolls

Status: accepted
Amends: [spec 0010](0010-professions.md) §9.2 (the mixed walker), as [amendment 1](0010-professions.amendment-1.md)
left it
Product owner: Zkalf (chose to keep both rules during plan 0012 M2, 2026-10-22)
Date: 2026-10-22

## Why
Plan 0012 M2's first run put every simulated walker at level 5 on day 3 (target day 1–2). At levels 3–4, with no
gear left to make, the walker crafted a copper pickaxe, hatchet and hammer: about 2,250 steps of ore, logs and
crafting at a quarter of a fight's XP. A copper pickaxe saves 10 steps per 100-step action, so it pays back only
after about 20 actions, more than the walker gathers that early. Rerolling Common gear costs 50 steps a time for no
XP, on gear that is replaced a few levels later.

## Change
§9.2, the mixed walker ("counts tools as upgrades, weighed by steps saved"; "rerolls its worn gear best-first while
it has 3 dust of the right quality") reads:
1. **Tools:** it crafts a tier's tools once its character is **2 levels past the tool recipe's level**: copper from
   level 5, iron from 14, mithril from 24, and only when no gear upgrade waits.
2. **Rerolls:** it rerolls worn gear **above Common**, best first, while it has 3 dust of the right quality, and a
   Legendary's weakest bonus stat (keeping the better roll). It doesn't reroll Common gear.

Real players have neither rule: they can craft a pickaxe on day 1 and reroll anything.
