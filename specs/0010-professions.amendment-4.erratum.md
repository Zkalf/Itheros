# Spec 0010 amendment 4, erratum 1: the tool line

Status: accepted
Corrects: [amendment 4](0010-professions.amendment-4.md) Change 4 and acceptance criterion 5
Product owner: Zkalf
Date: 2026-10-23

## What was wrong
Amendment 4 says "a tool line opens its profession's screen". No such link exists: the only tool line is on the
profession's own card, and it opens the tool's item card (§8.1, unchanged). Claude's wording mistake.

## Correction
- Change 4 reads: "the Forge row in Now opens Crafting's screen, and the Camp fire row opens Cooking's." The tool
  line stays on the profession's screen and still opens the tool's item card.
- Criterion 5 reads: "The Forge row and the Camp fire row open the matching profession's screen."
