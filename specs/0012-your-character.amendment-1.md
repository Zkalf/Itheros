# Spec 0012 amendment 1: the origin's profession reaches 10 sooner

Status: accepted
Amends: [spec 0012](0012-your-character.md) §5.2 (the walkers), and through it spec 0010 §9.3 target 2 as
[spec 0010 amendment 1](0010-professions.amendment-1.md) set it
Product owner: Zkalf (chose "lower the floor" during plan 0014 M1, 2026-10-22)
Date: 2026-10-22

## Why
Plan 0014 M1 made the simulated walkers Miner's children, so Mining starts at level 5. The walker then reaches
Mining 10 on day 6–7 (median 6), under target 2's floor of day 8. The head start is doing what it was built to do;
nothing else moved past an edge, so nothing is tuned.

## Change
Target 2 for **the origin's profession** (Mining, for the walkers): level 10 by day **5–22** (median). Level 20
keeps day 45–65. Crafting, which the walkers don't start with, keeps 8–22 and 45–65. Monsters, meals and XP tables
stay as they are.

## Numbers at the end of M1 (5,000 steps a day, seeds 1–8, Miner's child)
Level 10 on day 14–16, level 30 on day 78–97 (median 90.5; 91.0 without meals); Mining 10 median day 6, Mining 20
57; Crafting 10 median day 8, Crafting 20 49.
