# Spec 0017 amendment 1: Master of a Trade asks for four professions

Status: accepted
Amends: [spec 0017](0017-achievements-rework.md) §2.3 (row 29), §3.1 (the kinds of criterion) and criterion 7
Product owner: Zkalf (chose "level 30 in all four gathering professions" on 2026-10-24)
Date: 2026-10-24

## Why
The sim of plan 0021 M1 shows that the average walker (5,000 steps a day) reaches level 30 in one profession by day
90 without a grind. So "reach level 30 in one profession" is not a feat.

## Change
1. Master of a Trade asks for **level 30 in Mining, Woodcutting, Fishing and Hunting**. Its reward stays +6 Hit.
2. The criterion "any profession at a level" of §3.1 becomes **"each of these professions at a level"**:

   | Kind | Example | Reads |
   |---|---|---|
   | Each of a list of professions, at least a level | reach level 30 in Mining, Woodcutting, Fishing and Hunting | the lowest level of the professions in the list |

3. Criterion 7 becomes: **"Each of these professions" is earned when each profession of the list reaches its
   level.**
4. Each other part of spec 0017 stays.
