# Spec 0020 amendment 3: the bank screen's tabs as buttons, Bank first

Status: accepted
Amends: [spec 0020](0020-item-bank.md) §3.2 rule 15 (the tabs Bag and Bank)
Issue: #220
Product owner: Zkalf (asked in the playtest of 2026-11-06)
Date: 2026-11-06

## Why
- On the bank screen, Bag and Bank are a plain tab bar: a teal word with a line under it. The Journal's tabs are
  buttons in boxes. The two screens look different for the same kind of choice.
- The player opens the bank to see what it holds, but the screen opens on the Bag tab.

## Change
1. The tabs of the bank screen are buttons in boxes on a plate, as the Journal's tabs are (spec 0014 §2.2, amendment
   4): the selected one has the selected look.
2. The order is **Bank**, then **Bag**. Bank stands on the left.
3. The screen opens on the Bank tab.
4. What stays: the words, the lists, the chips of the kinds, the cards, and Store, Take and Destroy.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The bank screen shows Bank on the left and Bag on the right, each in a button box.
2. The selected tab has the selected look of spec 0014 amendment 4; the other does not.
3. The bank screen opens on the Bank tab.
4. A tap on Bag shows the bag's items; a tap on Bank shows the bank's.
